⚡

📁

Click "Load unpacked" and select the extracted FocusGuard folder.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FocusGuard. Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch.

❓

What is FocusGuard and how does it work?

+

FocusGuard is a lightweight Chrome extension that helps you stay productive by blocking distracting websites. Simply add the websites that distract you, choose between temporary blocking (set a timer) or scheduled blocking (create recurring focus hours), and FocusGuard will automatically redirect you to a motivating focus page whenever you try to access those sites.

💰

Is FocusGuard completely free?

+

Yes, absolutely free! FocusGuard is 100% free with no hidden costs, no premium plans, and no data collection. We believe that focus and productivity should be accessible to everyone. Use it as much as you want, whenever you want.

🔒

Is my browsing data safe with FocusGuard?

+

Your privacy is our priority. FocusGuard stores all data locally on your device. We don't collect, track, or send any of your browsing data anywhere. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no cloud storage — just pure local focus protection. You remain in complete control of your data.

🔄

Will my blocks stay active after browser restart?

+

Yes! FocusGuard automatically saves all your blocking rules, schedules, and active sessions. Even if you restart your browser or computer, your focus settings remain intact and blocking continues seamlessly. No need to reconfigure — just pick up where you left off.

🌐

Can I block any website?

+

Absolutely! You can block any website domain — social media (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook), entertainment (Netflix, Reddit), news, gaming, shopping, or any other site that distracts you. FocusGuard works on all websites, giving you complete control over your digital environment.

📅

Can I set different schedules for different days?

+

Yes! With FocusGuard's scheduled blocking, you can create custom schedules for each day of the week. For example: block YouTube on weekdays from 7 PM to 10 PM, but allow it on weekends. You can even set overnight schedules (e.g., 11 PM to 2 AM). The flexibility is yours!
